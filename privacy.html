1. Introduction

Welcome to Holy Bible Book (“we,” “our,” or “us”). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you visit our website at holybiblebook.org.

By using our site, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the site.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to operate this service effectively:

  • Contact Form Submissions: When you use our Contact Us page, we collect your name, email address, and message content through Netlify Forms.
  • Browser Storage: We use your browser’s localStorage to save your personal preferences (such as reading progress, bookmarks, highlights, font size, and dark/light mode). This data stays on your device and is never sent to our servers.
  • Analytics: We may collect anonymous usage data such as page views and traffic sources through standard web analytics tools. No personally identifiable information is collected through analytics.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect only to:

  • Respond to contact form submissions and prayer requests
  • Improve the functionality and content of the website
  • Understand how visitors use the site to make it better
  • Process support through our Buy Me a Coffee ministry page

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

Our site uses browser localStorage (not cookies) to store your reading preferences locally on your device. This includes:

  • Reading plan progress and start dates
  • Bookmarked chapters
  • Highlighted verses
  • Font size and display preferences
  • Dark/light mode setting
  • Selected Bible translation version

All of this data is stored entirely on your device and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser’s site data. We do not have access to this information.

Our website is designed to be a safe, family-friendly resource. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through our contact form, please contact us and we will remove it promptly.
