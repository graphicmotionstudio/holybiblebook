The Heart Behind It

A Ministry Born from Faith

My name is Steve Miller. I’m a web designer, motion graphics creator, and lifelong believer. I built Holy Bible Book because I believe the Gospel has the power to change lives — not just some lives, but every life. And in a world where information is everywhere but truth is hard to find, I wanted to create a place where anyone could open their browser and find the Word of God waiting for them.

Whether you’re someone who grew up in church, someone who has never opened a Bible, someone going through the hardest season of their life, or someone simply searching for something more — this site was built for you.

The scripture tells us in Romans 10:17 that faith comes by hearing, and hearing by the Word of God. That’s why I added an Audio Bible, 26+ public domain translations, and a Bible game — so the Word can reach you however you receive it best.

This is not just a website. It is a mission. And every person who opens it, reads a verse, or shares it with a friend is part of spreading the Gospel to the ends of the earth.
